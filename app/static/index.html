How the model works

  

    
Observed
DataCo v5
118 products in 51 categories, with historical demand aggregated from 180,519 order lines. A deterministic sample of 6,000 anonymous orders covers the full source period.

    
Assumed
Scenario network
All 13 facilities, 962 product-specific lanes, their capacities, lead times, backup contracts and retailer assignments were built for this simulation. The source data has no real supplier network.

  

  
How impact is calculated

  
For each product, a maximum-flow solver compares what reaches retailers before and after the disruption. Retailer demand is capped and flow is conserved. Backup contracts are optional, and capacity is independent across products. Inventory, bills of materials and production steps are not modeled.

  
Exposure depth only limits which downstream facilities are listed. The flow calculation always uses the complete network. Modeled transit time is a shortest-path lower bound, not a delivery promise.
