ASSUMED
Scenario network v1
All 13 facilities, 962 product-specific lanes, capacities, lead times, backup contracts and retailer assignments are constructed for simulation.
How impact is calculated
For each product, a capacitated maximum-flow solver compares baseline delivery with the disrupted network. Retailer demand is capped and flow is conserved. Backup contracts are optional; capacity is independent across products. No inventory, bill of materials or production transformations are modeled.
Exposure depth bounds the downstream exploration. The flow solver uses the complete, fixed-size scenario network. Modeled transit is a shortest-path lower bound, not a delivery promise.
